## Project Insight
"The Qtr/Year Total Sales" Chart indicates that annual sales have consistently increased from 2014 through 2017. The letter half of each year( Q3 and Q4) typically shows higher performance,suggesting seasonal peaks.
In the "Sub category wise Quantity" chart;Binders,Paper and Furnishings are the top movers. These office essentials drive the highest transaction volumes for the company.
The "Segment Wise Quantity" donut chart reveals that the Consumer segment is the largest contributor to total units sold,followed by Corporate and Home Office.
The "Region Wise Profit" chart shows that West and East regions are the primary engines of profitability.These areas significantly outperform the Central and Soith regions in bottom-line contruibution.

## Final Conclusion
TO maximize future performance, the company should:
Double down on West and East regions where profit margin are highest
Optimize inventory for high-volume sub-categories like Binders and Paper to meet consistent demand.
Target the Consumer segment with specific loyalty or promotional programs,as they represent the bulk of the customer base.
